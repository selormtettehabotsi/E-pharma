Our Mission

Access to affordable medicines is a right, not a privilege. Yet many Ghanaians overpay for drugs simply because price information is hard to find. E-Pharma bridges that gap by publishing the official National Health Insurance Scheme (NHIS) approved prices for hundreds of drugs in a fast, easy-to-search format — completely free.

Whether you're a patient picking up a prescription, a caregiver managing a family member's treatment, or a health worker verifying costs, E-Pharma gives you the information you need in seconds.

About the NHIS Price List

The prices on E-Pharma are based on the National Health Insurance Scheme (NHIS) approved medicine price list published by Ghana's National Health Insurance Authority (NHIA). These are the maximum prices that NHIS-accredited pharmacies and facilities are permitted to charge for listed medicines.

If you believe you have been charged more than the listed price at an NHIS-accredited facility, you have the right to report it to the NHIA.

A Note on Accuracy

We do our best to keep the data up to date, but drug prices can change when the NHIA publishes a new price list. Always confirm the final price with your pharmacist. E-Pharma is an informational tool and is not a substitute for professional medical or pharmaceutical advice.
